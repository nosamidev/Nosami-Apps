BeatDown

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the BeatDown app for Android ("BeatDown", "the app") handles information. BeatDown is published by nosamidev ("we", "us"). If you have any question, write to kaylorgem@gmail.com.

In short

  • We collect no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in and no profile.
  • BeatDown has no internet access. The app does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app contains no advertising or analytics code.
  • Your settings stay on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

1. Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal information. We cannot see how you use the app, which durations you set or which music you play.

2. Information stored only on your device

To work, BeatDown saves a few things in its private storage on your phone. This data never leaves your device through the app:

  • Your preferences: theme, the last duration you set, the beat you chose, and the beat volume.
  • The countdown in progress: whether a timer is running or paused, and when it ends. This lets the countdown continue if Android closes the app or the phone restarts. It is deleted when the countdown ends.
  • Your own music track, if you choose one: a reference to the audio file and its file name (see section 3).
  • Review prompt counters: how many countdowns have finished and when the Google Play rating dialog was last shown, so it is not shown too often.

You can delete all of this at any time by uninstalling the app, or in Android Settings → Apps → BeatDown → Storage → Clear storage.

3. Music you choose

If you use "Choose from my music", Android's own file picker opens and you pick one audio file. BeatDown receives read-only access to that single file and plays it on your device. The file is not copied, modified or uploaded anywhere. BeatDown cannot see your other files. If you pick a different track, the app gives up access to the previous one. You can also revoke access by clearing the app's storage.

4. Device backups

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include BeatDown's preferences (section 2) in your device backup, which Google stores in your Google account. This is handled by Android and Google under Google's Privacy Policy, not by us, and we cannot access it. The countdown in progress is excluded from backups. You can turn backups off in your device settings.

5. Permissions

BeatDown asks Android for these permissions, only to run the timer:

PermissionWhy
NotificationsShow the time left while the timer runs, and the "Time's up" alert with its Stop button. You can refuse it: the timer still works.
Alarms & reminders (exact alarms)Wake the phone exactly when the countdown reaches zero, even in battery-saving mode.
Foreground serviceKeep the countdown running while the app is in the background.
Run at startupBring back a running countdown after the phone restarts.
Vibration, keep awakeVibrate when time is up, and keep the beat playing while the screen is off.
View network connectionsAdded by the audio library BeatDown uses. It only lets the app check whether a network is available, which BeatDown does not use. It does not give internet access.

BeatDown does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or phone identifiers.

6. Google Play

BeatDown is distributed through Google Play. After a few completed countdowns, the app may ask Google Play to show its standard rating dialog. That dialog is run entirely by Google Play. We do not see whether it was shown or what you answered. Any rating or review you post is public on Google Play under your Google Play name.

Google may collect information when you download or use apps from Google Play, for example crash and performance reports if you allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data. Google shares these reports with developers in aggregated form, without your name or contact details. This is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Children

BeatDown is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, it does not collect data from children either.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws (such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, Moroccan Law No. 09-08 on the protection of personal data, or California privacy law) give you rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete. Everything BeatDown stores is on your device and under your control (section 2). If you have a question about your rights, you can still contact us.

9. Security

BeatDown's data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and is protected by your device's own security (screen lock and encryption).

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version of BeatDown changes how it handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released. If a change means the app would start collecting personal data, we will say so clearly in the app before it happens.
