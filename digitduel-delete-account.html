DigitDuel is developed by Amine Naciri. You can delete your DigitDuel account and its data in two ways.

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open DigitDuel.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete My Account.
  4. Confirm with Yes, Delete My Account.

Your data is deleted right away. If you signed in with Google, the app may ask you to sign in once more and delete again, for security.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the app)

Send an email to nosami.dev.21@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include:

  • Google accounts: send it from the email address you signed in with.
  • Guest accounts: your username and, if you know it, your friend code (Profile or Friends → Add friend). Guest accounts have no email address, so we may ask for more details to confirm the account is yours.

We confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login (Google link or guest account) and email address
  • Your username, public profile and friend code
  • Your level, XP, rank, statistics, achievements, titles, cosmetics and daily-challenge record
  • Your match history and season leaderboard entry
  • Your friends list, the friend requests you sent or received, and your game invites

What may remain

  • Records of past online matches that belong to your opponents (their match history, and the room and round records of games you played together). These are linked only to a random account ID that no longer belongs to anyone, and they contain no email address.
  • Copies in our provider's (Google Firebase) backup systems, for a limited period until they are overwritten.
  • Information we must keep by law, if any.

You can also delete only part of your data without deleting your account: change your username in Profile, or remove friends in Friends. For any other request, email us.
