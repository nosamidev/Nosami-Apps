Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Memory & Math Puzzles app for Android ("the app") handles information. The app is published by NosamiDev ("we", "us"). If you have any question, write to nosami.dev.21@gmail.com.

In short

  • No account, no sign-in, no profile. We do not collect your name, email or any personal information.
  • Your games stay on your device. Scores, personal bests, history, puzzle progress and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. The ads SDK may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads (section 6). In the EEA and the UK you are asked for consent first.
  • No analytics and no crash reporting of our own. We cannot see how you play.

1. Who we are

NosamiDev is an independent developer. For any privacy question or request, contact nosami.dev.21@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We (NosamiDev) do not collect, receive, store or sell any personal information. The app has no server of its own and never sends your scores or progress to us. The only data that leaves your device is the data handled by Google's advertising and consent SDKs, described in sections 6 and 7.

3. Information stored only on your device

To work, the app saves a few things in its private storage on your phone:

  • Your settings: language, vibration on/off, daily reminder on/off.
  • Your results: personal bests, the history of recent scores (used for the charts in Progress), today's Workout progress and which puzzles you have solved.
  • Counters: the number of free and ad-rewarded hints used today, and how many sessions you have completed (so no full-screen ad is shown in your first sessions, and ads are spaced out).
  • Your ad consent choice, stored by Google's User Messaging Platform.

You can delete all of this at any time by uninstalling the app, or in Android Settings → Apps → Memory & Math → Storage → Clear storage.

4. Permissions

The app asks Android for the following permissions. Some are added by Google's libraries.

PermissionWhy
POST_NOTIFICATIONSShows the optional daily reminder. Asked only when you turn on Settings → Daily reminder. The reminder is created on your device; nothing is sent.
VIBRATEShort vibration on a correct or wrong answer. Can be turned off in Settings → Vibration.
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEUsed only by Google AdMob and the consent SDK to load ads and the consent form. The games themselves work offline.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_IDLets AdMob read the Android advertising ID to show and measure ads (section 6).
ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICSAdded by the AdMob SDK for Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising APIs, on devices that support them.
WAKE_LOCKAdded by Android's WorkManager library so the daily reminder check can finish while the phone is asleep.

The app does not use your location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Play distributes the app: Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) shows ads: How Google uses information in ads.
  • Google User Messaging Platform shows the ad consent form where required.

6. Advertising

The app is free and is funded by ads from Google AdMob. Two kinds of ads are shown, never during a game or a puzzle:

  • a full-screen ad, at most once every few minutes, when you leave a finished Workout session or a completed puzzle pack (never in your first two sessions);
  • an optional rewarded ad, only if you tap "Watch an ad for 1 extra hint" after using your 3 free daily hints.

To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process: the advertising ID and other device identifiers, device information (model, operating system, language), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad interactions (views, clicks) and diagnostic data (crash and performance data of the SDK). This data is processed by Google under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy. Data is encrypted in transit.

Ad content is limited to the "PG" maturity rating. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).

7. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in a region where consent is required, the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can choose personalised ads, non-personalised ads, or manage each purpose. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy options.

8. Children

The app is intended for teenagers and adults (13 and older) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and you want ad-related data handled, contact us at nosami.dev.21@gmail.com; you can also reset the device's advertising ID and clear the app's storage as described above.

9. Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's local data (settings and progress) in your device backup and restore it on a new phone. This backup is handled by Google, not by us. You can turn it off in Android Settings → System → Backup.

10. Security

Your game data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing of yours stored on our side.

11. Your rights and deletion

All data the app keeps is on your device and is deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's Privacy Policy and your Google account settings. We cannot delete data held by Google on your behalf, but you can write to us with any question.

12. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
