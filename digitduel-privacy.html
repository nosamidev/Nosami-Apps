This policy explains what information the DigitDuel mobile game ("DigitDuel", "the app") collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. DigitDuel is developed and operated by Amine Naciri, based in Morocco ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the information described here.

Contact: nosami.dev.21@gmail.com

Summary

  • We collect only what is needed to run the game: an account identifier, the username you play under, your game progress, your online matches, and your friends list.
  • If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and a Google account identifier. We do not use or store your Google name or profile photo.
  • Your username, level, rank, title, cosmetics and friend code are visible to other players, and players can find you by username or friend code.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no advertising ID, no location, no contacts, no payments. We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time in the app (Profile → Delete My Account) or by email.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account information

Data When Source
Account ID (a random identifier) Every account Firebase Authentication
Email address and Google account identifier Only if you sign in with Google Google, via Firebase Authentication
Username You choose it as a guest. With Google, a neutral one is generated (e.g. Player123456), and you can change it. You

Guest accounts are not tied to an email address. If you uninstall the app or clear its data while playing as a guest, you lose access to that account, because there is no way to sign back in to it.

1.2 Game profile and progress

Level, experience points (XP), rank, statistics (wins, losses, draws, games played, streaks, best number of attempts, fastest wins and the dates of your wins), achievements, titles and cosmetic items you unlocked or equipped, season points and season badges, the date of your last completed daily challenge, and recent results against computer opponents (used to adjust their difficulty).

1.3 Match information

  • Online matches: the room code and settings, your secret code and guesses, round timing, and "last active" timestamps. The timestamps let us detect when a player has disconnected, so the match can be awarded fairly.
  • Match history: date, result, number of attempts, the opponent's secret code (revealed at the end of the match), code length, duration, XP earned and match type.

1.4 Friends and invites

Your friend code; your friends list and the date you became friends; friend requests you send or receive (sender, recipient, status, date); and game invites (sender, recipient, room, settings, status). An invite can only be accepted within 60 seconds.

1.5 Information stored only on your device

Sound and vibration settings, whether you completed the introduction, and an encrypted save of an unfinished match against a computer opponent so you can resume it. This stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

1.6 Technical information

To provide sign-in and online play, our service providers (Google Firebase and Google Play services) process technical data such as your IP address, device and operating system type, and app version. We do not use this to build profiles or for advertising.

1.7 What we do not collect

Real name or Google profile photo, phone number, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos or files, payment information, or the advertising ID. DigitDuel contains no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

3. What other players can see

  • Anyone signed in to DigitDuel: your username, level, rank, title, equipped cosmetics, guest/Google account type, and friend code. Players can find you by searching your username or entering your friend code.
  • Season leaderboard: your username, title and season points.
  • Opponents in a match: your username, the feedback on your guesses and, at the end of the match, your secret code.
  • Friends: your profile as above, and game invites you send them.

Your email address is never shown to other players. Choose a username that does not reveal personal information.

Computer opponents: when no player is available within a few seconds, Find Match pairs you with a computer opponent. Matches against computer opponents can count toward your statistics and progress.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

  • Service providers (processors): Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd., for Firebase Authentication (sign-in), Cloud Firestore (data storage) and Google Play services (Google sign-in). They process data on our behalf under Google's data-processing terms. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Other players: only the information listed in section 3.
  • Legal reasons: when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of players, the public or us.
  • Change of ownership: if the app is transferred to a new owner, your information may transfer with it under this policy, and we will tell you.

5. International transfers

Google stores and processes data in data centres in several countries, including the United States and the European Union. When data leaves your country, the transfer is protected by Google's safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep information

  • Account, profile, progress, friends and history: until you delete your account.
  • Game invites: they expire after 60 seconds and are deleted along with your account.
  • Guest accounts: kept until deleted. We may delete guest accounts that have been inactive for a long time.
  • Shared match records: an online match is a record shared by both players. After you delete your account, the copies of past matches that belong to your opponents (their match history, and the room and round records) remain. They are linked only to a random account ID that no longer belongs to anyone, and they never contain your email address.
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in our providers' backup systems for a limited period until it is overwritten.

7. Your rights and choices

  • In the app: view your stats and history, change your username, remove friends, and delete your account (Profile → Delete My Account).
  • By email (nosami.dev.21@gmail.com): ask for a copy of your data, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or a portable copy (data portability).
  • EU/UK residents: you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • California residents: you have the right to know, delete and correct your personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not use sensitive personal information.

We answer within 30 days. To protect your account, we may ask you to confirm the request from the email address linked to your Google account, or to give your username and friend code (guest accounts).

9. Children

DigitDuel is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 years old, or older if the law of your country requires a higher age to use online services without parental consent. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Access to stored data is restricted by server-side security rules, so players can only read and change what they are allowed to. The unfinished-match save on your device is encrypted. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

We will post any update on this page and change the "Last updated" date. If the changes are significant, we will also tell you in the app.
